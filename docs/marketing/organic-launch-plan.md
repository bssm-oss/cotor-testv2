# Organic Launch Plan

## Overview
This document outlines the organic launch strategy focusing exclusively on owned channels: website and social media. No external posting or paid strategies are included.

## Owned Website Strategy

### Pre-Launch Phase
- Update product pages with launch messaging
- Create dedicated landing page for new feature
- Implement SEO optimizations for launch keywords
- Add announcement banner to homepage
- Prepare FAQ section addressing anticipated user questions

### Launch Phase
- Publish launch blog post detailing features and benefits
- Update product documentation with new feature information
- Release changelog entry highlighting launch
- Feature announcement in website header/navigation
- Create tutorial/demo videos for embedded site content

### Post-Launch Phase
- Monitor website analytics for engagement metrics
- Collect user feedback through on-site surveys
- Iterate on landing page based on user behavior
- Publish follow-up content addressing common use cases
- Maintain updated documentation as features evolve

## Owned Social Messaging Strategy

### Platform-Specific Approach

#### Twitter/X
- Thread announcement detailing launch journey
- Feature spotlight tweets with visuals/gifs
- Engage with comments and questions in real-time
- Retweet user testimonials and feedback
- Share behind-the-scenes development insights

#### LinkedIn
- Professional announcement post targeting business audience
- Case study format posts showing value proposition
- Employee advocacy sharing from team members
- Industry-relevant hashtags and tagging
- Long-form article explaining technical benefits

#### Facebook
- Community-focused announcement in relevant groups
- Visual storytelling with before/after scenarios
- Live Q&A session scheduling
- Customer success story highlights
- Event creation for virtual launch gathering

#### Instagram
- Visual carousel posts showcasing features
- Reels demonstrating product in action
- Story series with interactive polls/questions
- User-generated content features
- Behind-the-scenes team preparation content

### Content Calendar
- Week 1: Teaser content building anticipation
- Week 2: Launch announcement across all platforms
- Week 3: Feature deep-dives and tutorials
- Week 4: User testimonials and case studies
- Ongoing: Regular engagement and value-driven posts

### Engagement Guidelines
- Respond to all comments within 2 business hours
- Maintain consistent brand voice across platforms
- Use platform-native features (stories, reels, threads)
- Avoid cross-posting identical content
- Track engagement metrics per platform

## Success Metrics
- Website traffic increase to launch pages
- Social media engagement rates (likes, shares, comments)
- Time on site for launch-related content
- Conversion rates from announcement to product pages
- Follower growth during launch period
- Sentiment analysis of social mentions

## Content Pillars
1. Education: How-to guides and feature explanations
2. Inspiration: Use case demonstrations and success stories
3. Community: User engagement and conversation starters
4. Transparency: Development process and team insights
5. Value: Clear articulation of benefits and outcomes

## Implementation Timeline
- T-2 weeks: Content creation and scheduling
- T-1 week: Pre-launch teasers and preparation
- T=0: Launch day coordinated announcement
- T+1 week: Amplification and engagement focus
- T+2 weeks: Optimization based on initial response

## Approval Process
All content to be reviewed by:
- Marketing lead for messaging consistency
- Product team for technical accuracy
- Legal/compliance for regulatory adherence
- Design team for visual brand alignment

## Resources Required
- Content creation team (copy, design, video)
- Social media management tools
- Analytics tracking setup
- Community management personnel
- Executive sponsorship for amplification

---
*This plan focuses solely on owned website and social media channels as requested. No external posting, paid advertising, or third-party promotional strategies are included.*
